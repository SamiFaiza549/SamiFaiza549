## 📝 About Me

I’m a Computer Science undergraduate at the Asian University for Women (AUW) with 3+ years of experience in teaching, research, and project coordination. Currently, I work as a Teaching Assistant for Stanford University's Code in Place and at AUW, helping students strengthen their programming skills.

I have a research focus in Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Natural Language Processing (NLP), gaining hands-on experience as a Research Assistant. Additionally, I serve as an Assistant Project Coordinator at Reflective Teens - RT, where I support tech-driven community projects aimed at making a real-world impact.

I'm passionate about using technology to create sustainable solutions and always open to new opportunities in research, collaboration, or tech-for-good initiatives.
